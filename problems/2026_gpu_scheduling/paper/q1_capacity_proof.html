S6. GPU-work weighted waiting: a sample-path lower bound

For each original isolated model pool m, use the finite, complete supplied job set 𝒥m, fixed integer GPU capacity CmG, and fixed demands and durations. Let Gj = Wjgj, Hj = Gjdj (GPU-seconds), and ωj = sj − rj ≥ 0. W still denotes workers. Consider any legal nonpreemptive schedule π completing these jobs, with no model migration. Define:

Fm0(t) = ∫0tDm(u)du,
Fmπ(t) = Σj∈𝒥m Gj clip(t − sj, 0, dj).

Sample-path identity. For a service offset v ∈ [0,dj], the immediately started ramp includes that unit of service from rj+v onwards, whereas the scheduled ramp includes it only from sj+v. Integrating their nonnegative indicator difference over t yields sj−rj. Integrating over v and multiplying by Gj yields Hjωj. Summing the finite set (or applying Tonelli) proves:

Σ Hjωj = ∫0∞[Fm0(t) − Fmπ(t)]dt.

Capacity relaxation. Since sj ≥ rj, Fmπ(u) ≤ Fm0(u). Capacity validity gives, for every 0 ≤ u ≤ t, Fmπ(t) ≤ Fmπ(u) + CmG(t−u) ≤ Fm0(u) + CmG(t−u). Thus:

Fm0(t) − Fmπ(t) ≥ Bm(t)
= sup0≤u≤t{Fm0(t) − Fm0(u) − CmG(t−u)},
Ωmπ = Σ Hjωj / Σ Hj ≥ Lm = ∫0∞Bm(t)dt / Σ Hj.

The choice u=t ensures B≥0. CPU, node shape and gang constraints are relaxed, so the bound also applies to legal schedules respecting them. Areas have units GPU·s²; dividing by GPU·s gives seconds. Divide by 3600 only to display hours. With zero total work we output L=0 by convention; the weighted mean itself is undefined.

Exact full-lifetime calculation. Merge all arrival/end deltas at identical timestamps, including the time-zero submission batch. In each half-open interval of length Δ with constant immediate-start demand q and initial backlog b, integrate max(0,b+(q−C)x). If backlog drains inside the interval, use the exact triangle b²/[2(C−q)] rather than an endpoint trapezoid across the whole interval. Otherwise the trapezoid is exact. At t*=max(rj+dj), add the remaining tail b²/(2C); the fluid is empty at t*+b/C. This is not a scheduler makespan. Python integers and rational fractions protect squared products from int64 overflow. No daily averaging or sampling is used. The old observation-window statistics retain T=max rj, independently of this infinite-horizon integral.

Interpretation and limits. GPU model names alone do not identify training, inference or application compatibility. Ω is GPU-work weighted waiting, not ordinary mean, per-job or maximum waiting, and not wasted GPU-hours. Zero L does not imply a feasible zero-wait schedule. The bound need not be tight, does not prove optimality, and implies no conservation of waiting across policies. Original-pool values do not transfer to Q3 with changed capacities, durations or model migration, nor to a subset of jobs. The external 560.3 h prototype agrees with the computed pre-tail value at its reported precision; without its source, a missing-tail code bug is not established. Agreement with external numbers does not resolve historical raw byte identity.

繁中說明。把每個作業的累計服務坡形向右移 ωj，兩條曲線之間的面積恰為 Hjωj。合法分配既不能早於提交，又不能超過 GPU 容量，因此累計服務缺口不小於反射流體積壓；積分後得到工作量加權等待下界。CPU、節點形狀與 gang 約束在此放鬆。積分包含最後立即完成事件之後的排空三角形；流體排空時間不是排程 makespan。本結果不是普通平均、個別或最大等待下界，不是浪費的 GPU-hours；零下界不代表可零等待，也不證明緊性、最優或等待守恆。Q3 改容量、時長或跨型號後須重算；子集實驗不能引用全量 A10 下界。
